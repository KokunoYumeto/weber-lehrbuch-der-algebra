We make one further application of the transformation of the second order, to the proof of a formula which is necessary for the transformation of odd order.

In the second equation (10) we replace $\omega$ by $2\omega$, so that:
\[
2\vartheta_{00}(u,\,2\omega)\vartheta_{10}(u,\,2\omega) = \vartheta_{10}\vartheta_{10}(u).
\]
By this we multiply the second equation (11), so that we obtain
\[
2\vartheta_{01}(0,\,2\omega)\vartheta_{01}(2u,\,2\omega)\vartheta_{00}(u,\,2\omega)\vartheta_{10}(u,\,2\omega)
= \vartheta_{10}\vartheta_{10}(u)\vartheta_{00}(u)\vartheta_{01}(u).
\]
This we divide by the product of the two equations (7), (8):
\[
2\vartheta_{00}(0,\,2\omega)\vartheta_{10}(0,\,2\omega)\vartheta_{01}(0,\,2\omega)^2 = \vartheta_{10}^2\vartheta_{00}\vartheta_{01}
\]
and obtain

\weq{18}{\dfrac{\vartheta_{00}(u,\,2\omega)\vartheta_{10}(u,\,2\omega)\vartheta_{01}(2u,\,2\omega)}{\vartheta_{00}(0,\,2\omega)\vartheta_{10}(0,\,2\omega)\vartheta_{01}(0,\,2\omega)} = \dfrac{\vartheta_{00}(u)\vartheta_{10}(u)\vartheta_{01}(u)}{\vartheta_{00}\vartheta_{10}\vartheta_{01}}.}

If now $n$ denotes any \gesperrt{odd} integer, then the function
\[
\vartheta_{01}\left(\frac{\nu}{n}\right),
\]
remains unchanged when $\nu$ increases by a multiple of $n$, and consequently the product
\[
\overset{\nu}{\prod}\ \vartheta_{01}\left(\frac{\nu}{n}\right),
\]
when it is taken over a complete system of residues with respect to the modulus $n$, is independent of the particular choice of this system of residues. Therefore, since $2\nu$ runs through such a system of residues at the same time as $\nu$,

\weq{19}{\prod_{1,\,n-1}^{\nu} \vartheta_{01}\left(\frac{\nu}{n}\right) = \prod_{1,\,n-1}^{\nu} \vartheta_{01}\left(\frac{2\nu}{n}\right).}

If therefore we set $u = \nu:n$ in (18), form the product, and make use of formula (19) in the last factor of the left side, it follows that the product

\weq{20}{\dfrac{\displaystyle\prod_{1,\,n-1}^{\nu} \vartheta_{00}\left(\dfrac{\nu}{n}\right)\vartheta_{10}\left(\dfrac{\nu}{n}\right)\vartheta_{01}\left(\dfrac{\nu}{n}\right)}{\vartheta_{00}^{n-1}\vartheta_{10}^{n-1}\vartheta_{01}^{n-1}}}

remains unchanged when $\omega$ is replaced by $2\omega$.
