If we set
\[
k = k'\Delta'' + k''\Delta',
\]
and let $k'$, $k''$ run through systems of residues with respect to $\Delta'$, $\Delta''$, then $k$ runs through a system of residues with respect to $\Delta = \Delta'\Delta''$.

The multiplication of the two sums
\weq{4}{\overset{k'}{\Sigma}(\Delta',k')\,e^{-\frac{2s\pi ik'}{\Delta'}}, \quad \overset{k''}{\Sigma}(\Delta'',k'')\,e^{-\frac{2s\pi ik''}{\Delta''}}}
gives
\weq{5}{\overset{k'k''}{\Sigma}(\Delta',k')(\Delta'',k'')\,e^{-\frac{2s\pi ik}{\Delta}}.}

But by \S\,85, (16) we have
\[
(\Delta',k) = (\Delta',\Delta'')(\Delta',k'),
\]
\[
(\Delta'',k) = (\Delta'',\Delta')(\Delta'',k''),
\]
and consequently, by \S\,85, (13),
\[
(\Delta,k) = \pm(\Delta',k')(\Delta'',k''),
\]
and accordingly the general validity of formula (2) for every fundamental discriminant $\Delta$ follows from (3), (4) and (5).
