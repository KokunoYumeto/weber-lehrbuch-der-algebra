The most general integral (2) belonging to the fundamental curve passes,
through the introduction of homogeneous variables $z = x_3 : x_4$, into the
following form:
\[
\int F'(x,y,z)\,\frac{x_3\,dx_4 - x_4\,dx_3}{x_4^2},
\]
or, if one sets
\[
F(x,y,z) = x_4^2\,\frac{F(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)}{\varphi_1\psi_2 - \varphi_2\psi_1}
\]
it passes into
\[
\int F(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)\,\frac{x_3\,dx_4 - x_4\,dx_3}{\varphi_1\psi_2 - \varphi_2\psi_1},
\]
in which $F$ is a homogeneous function of degree 0. The simplest case is
$F=1$, and so we consider the integral of the first kind:
\weq{26}{u = \int \frac{x_3\,dx_4 - x_4\,dx_3}{\varphi_1\psi_2 - \varphi_2\psi_1}\,.}

Now, by the multiplication theorem of determinants, we form the
product $K(x_3\,dx_4 - x_2\,dx_4)$, % [sic] printed x_3 dx_4 - x_2 dx_4; the determinant and (26) require x_3 dx_4 - x_4 dx_3
and hence, since $\varphi=0$, $\psi=0$:
\[
\begin{vmatrix}
\varphi_1, & \varphi_2, & \varphi_3, & \varphi_4 \\
\psi_1, & \psi_2, & \psi_3, & \psi_4 \\
\varPhi_1, & \varPhi_2, & \varPhi_3, & \varPhi_4 \\
\varPsi_1, & \varPsi_2, & \varPsi_3, & \varPsi_4
\end{vmatrix}
\begin{vmatrix}
1, & 0, & 0, & 0 \\
0, & 1, & 0, & 0 \\
x_1, & x_2, & x_3, & x_4 \\
dx_1, & dx_2, & dx_3, & dx_4
\end{vmatrix}
=
\begin{vmatrix}
\varphi_1, & \varphi_2, & 0, & 0 \\
\psi_1, & \psi_2, & 0, & 0 \\
\varPhi_1, & \varPhi_2, & \varPhi, & d\varPhi \\
\varPsi_1, & \varPsi_2, & \varPsi, & d\varPsi
\end{vmatrix}
\]
\[
= (\varphi_1\psi_2 - \varphi_2\psi_1)(\varPhi\,d\varPsi - \varPsi\,d\varPhi),
\]
and consequently there results from (26) and (23)
\weq{27}{u = \int \frac{\varPhi\,d\varPsi - \varPsi\,d\varPhi}{K} = \int \frac{\varPhi\,d\varPsi - \varPsi\,d\varPhi}{\sqrt{f(\varPhi,-\varPsi)}}\,.}

If one applies to this the transformation of \S\,5, replacing $x,y$
by $\varPhi, -\varPsi$, there follows
\[
u = \sqrt{3}\int \frac{f\,dH - H\,df}{\sqrt{-(H^3 - 48\,Af^2H - 64\,Bf^3)f}},
\]
and if in \S\,5, (17) one takes $\mu = 3$, so that
\[
z = -\frac{3H}{4f}
\]
one obtains:
\[
u = 3\int \frac{dz}{\sqrt{4z^3 - g_2 z - g_3}},
\]
\[
g_2 = 12\cdot 9\,A, \qquad g_3 = -4\cdot 27\,B.
\]
