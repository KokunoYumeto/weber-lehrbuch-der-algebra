\begin{center}
§ 10. \gesperrt{The Transformation of the Third Degree.}
\end{center}

As a second example we consider the transformation of the third degree.
The equation

\weq{1}{UV(U-V)(U-\lambda^2V) = T^2\xi\eta(\xi-\eta)(\xi-\varkappa^2\eta)}

requires that each of the four factors of the third degree $U$, $V$,
$U-V$, $U-\lambda^2V$ be divisible by one of the linear factors $\xi$,
$\eta$, $\xi-\eta$, $\xi-\varkappa^2\eta$, and that the quotient be a square.
We therefore set

\weq{2}{U = \xi(a\xi+b\eta)^2, \qquad V = \eta(a'\xi+b'\eta)^2,}

and require further that

\weq{3}{\frac{U-V}{\xi-\eta}, \qquad \frac{U-\lambda^2V}{\xi-\varkappa^2\eta}}

become the squares of linear functions.

Of the last two requirements the one follows from the other if we so
arrange $U$, $V$ that, apart from constant factors, $U$ and $V$ pass into
one another under the interchange of $\xi$, $\eta$ with $\varkappa^2\eta$,
$\xi$. But under this interchange

\[
\xi(a\xi+b\eta)^2, \qquad \eta(a'\xi+b'\eta)^2
\]

pass over into

\[
\varkappa^2\eta(b\xi+a\varkappa^2\eta)^2, \qquad \xi(b'\xi+a'\varkappa^2\eta)^2,
\]

and our requirement is satisfied if

\weq{4}{\varkappa^2 = \frac{bb'}{aa'}}

holds. If then

\weq{5}{\lambda^2 = \varkappa^2\left(\frac{ab}{a'b'}\right)^2}

holds, then by this interchange

\weq{6}{\frac{U-V}{\xi-\eta} \ \text{passes over into} \ \frac{b'^2}{a^2}\,\frac{U-\lambda^2V}{\xi-\varkappa^2\eta}}

Now that this has been laid down, only the condition remains to be
satisfied that the first of the two quantities (3) becomes the square of a
linear function, which we denote by $(\alpha^2\xi-\beta^2\eta)$, that is:

\weq{7}{U-V = (\xi-\eta)(\alpha^2\xi-\beta^2\eta)^2}

or

\[
U-\xi(\alpha^2\xi-\beta^2\eta)^2 = V-\eta(\alpha^2\xi-\beta^2\eta)^2,
\]
