We have assumed the convergence of this series for all values of $v$;
in order to judge of this, we observe that the general term of this
 series becomes
\[
\begin{array}{lccl}
\text{for} & \nu = +\infty & \text{equal to} & -e^{2\pi i\nu a}\\
\text{\textquotedbl} & \nu = -\infty & \text{\textquotedbl} & e^{-2\pi iv}e^{-2\pi i\nu(\omega-a)}
\end{array}
\]
Convergence will therefore take place if the imaginary
part of $a$ and of $\omega - a$ is positive. If we therefore set
$\omega = \omega' + i\omega''$, $a = a' + ia''$, then the condition of convergence is

\weq{8}{0 < a'' < \omega''.}
If in (7) one replaces $a$ by $a + \frac{1}{2}$, there results the
development for a second of the 16 functions:
\weq{9}{\frac{\vartheta'_{11}\vartheta_{10}(v+a)}{2\pi i\vartheta_{11}(v)\vartheta_{10}(a)} = \sum^{\nu} \frac{(-1)^\nu e^{2\pi ia\nu}}{q^{2\nu}e^{2\pi iv} - 1},}
and if in (7) and (9) one changes $a$ into $a + \frac{1}{2}\omega$:
\weq{10}{\frac{\vartheta'_{11}\vartheta_{01}(v+a)}{2\pi i\vartheta_{11}(v)\vartheta_{01}(a)} = \sum^{\nu} \frac{e^{2\pi ia\nu}q^\nu e^{\pi iv}}{q^{2\nu}e^{2\pi iv} - 1},}
\weq{11}{\frac{\vartheta'_{11}\vartheta_{00}(v+a)}{2\pi i\vartheta_{11}(v)\vartheta_{00}(a)} = \sum^{\nu} \frac{(-1)^\nu e^{2\pi ia\nu}q^\nu e^{\pi iv}}{q^{2\nu}e^{2\pi iv} - 1},}
where it is however to be noted that in the last two formulae
the condition of convergence is altered, namely:
\[
-\frac{\omega''}{2} < a'' < \frac{\omega''}{2}.
\]
From the four formulae the remaining twelve follow if we
replace $v$ by $v + \frac{1}{2}$, $v + \frac{1}{2}\omega$, $v + \frac{1}{2}(1+\omega)$, whereby the
domains of convergence are not further altered. In this way
the formulae of Table I at the end of the volume are derived.

The series so obtained do not all converge for real values
of $a$; the series (7), for example, does not, since in it the part
running towards the side of the positive $\nu$
\weq{12}{\sum_{1,\infty}^{\nu} \frac{e^{2\pi i\nu a}}{q^{2\nu}e^{2\pi iv} - 1}}
ceases to converge when the imaginary part of $a$ becomes equal
to zero, while the other part is still convergent even there
