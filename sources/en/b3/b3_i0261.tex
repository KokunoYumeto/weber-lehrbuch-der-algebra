2. The function
\weq{17}{F_n[x, j(\omega)] = \Pi\left[x - j\left(\frac{c+\partial\omega}{a}\right)\right]}
has a finite value for every finite value of $\omega$ with positive imaginary part, and hence also for every \gesperrt{finite value} of $j(\omega)$, and is accordingly a \gesperrt{polynomial} in $j(\omega)$.

If we next seek, by (3), the initial term of the development
of the function (17) in powers of $q$, we obtain
\[
(-1)^{\nu} e^{-2\pi i \Sigma \frac{c}{a}} e^{-2\pi i \omega \Sigma \frac{\partial}{e}\varphi(e)} = C q^{-2\nu},
\]
where $C$ is a finite constant. Therefore
\[
j(\omega)^{-\nu} F_n[x, j(\omega)]
\]
is finite for an infinite $j(\omega)$, that is, the degree of $F_n[x, j(\omega)]$
with respect to $j(\omega)$ is likewise the $\nu$th.
