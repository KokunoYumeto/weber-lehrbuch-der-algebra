3. For the case $m \equiv 7\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 8)$ we cannot
determine the value of $\tau$ in this simple way. For this the limit
formula derived in the preceding section is required. We first treat
the two cases $m \equiv 3\ (\mathrm{mod}\ 4)$ uniformly and set:
\[
\varphi = ax^2 + bxy + cy^2 = (a,b,c),
\]
\weq{8}{b^2 - 4ac = -m,}
\weq{9}{S' = \sum^{x,y}\frac{1}{\varphi(x,y)^s},}
in which $x, y$ run through all integer values, with the exception of
the combination $0, 0$.

% [sic] Printed unprimed S refers to the sum S' defined in (9) and decomposed in (10).
We decompose the sum $S$ into four partial sums
\[
S_{00}', \quad S_{01}', \quad S_{10}', \quad S_{11}',
\]
so that $x, y$ run through only even values in $S_{00}'$, and only odd
values in $S_{11}'$. In $S_{10}'$, $x$ runs through the odd numbers and
$y$ the even ones, and conversely in $S_{01}'$. Then
\weq{10}{S' + 2S_{00}' = (S_{01}'+S_{00}') + (S_{10}'+S_{00}') + (S_{11}'+S_{00}').}

If we replace
\[
\begin{array}{l@{\quad}l@{\quad}l@{\quad}c@{\quad}l}
\text{in} & S_{00}' & x,y & \text{by} & 2x,\ 2y,\\
\text{\glqq} & S_{01}'+S_{00}' & x,y & \text{\glqq} & 2x,\ y,\\
\text{\glqq} & S_{10}'+S_{00}' & x,y & \text{\glqq} & x,\ 2y,\\
\text{\glqq} & S_{00}'+S_{11}' & x,y & \text{\glqq} & x-y,\ x+y,
\end{array}
\]
then the new $x, y$ are subject to no further restriction than that they
do not both vanish at the same time. If we therefore set:
\weq{11}{\begin{aligned}
\varphi &= (a,b,c),\\
\varphi_1 &= (4a, 2b, c),\\
\varphi_2 &= (a, 2b, 4c),\\
\varphi_3 &= (a+b+c,\ 2(c-a),\ a-b+c),
\end{aligned}}
then
\weq{12}{\begin{aligned}
4^s S_{00}' &= S',\\
S_{01}' + S_{00}' &= \overset{x,y}{\Sigma} \varphi_1^{-s},\\
S_{10}' + S_{00}' &= \overset{x,y}{\Sigma} \varphi_2^{-s},\\
S_{00}' + S_{11}' &= \overset{x,y}{\Sigma} \varphi_3^{-s}.
\end{aligned}}
