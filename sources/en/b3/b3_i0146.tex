The exact determination of this constant $\varepsilon$, especially with regard to the sign as well, is a well-known and important problem which presents peculiar difficulties; its solution, however, is indispensable for us. Solutions have been given along various routes by \gesperrt{Hermite}\footnote{Liouvilles Journal, Ser.\,II, T.\,III, 1858. Oeuvres de \gesperrt{Charles Hermite}, p.\,487.} and \gesperrt{Dedekind}\footnote{Erläuterungen zu Nr.\,XXVIII von \gesperrt{Riemanns Werken}, second edition, and \glqq{}Über die elliptischen Modulfunktionen\grqq{}, Crelles Journal, Bd.\,83, S.\,265. Cf. also the author's memoir \glqq{}Zur Theorie der elliptischen Funktionen\grqq{}, Acta Mathematica, Bd.\,6, S.\,341\,ff.}, and more recently also by \gesperrt{Mertens} and \gesperrt{Scheibner}\footnote{\gesperrt{Mertens}, Zur linearen Transformation der $\vartheta$-Reihen, Transactions of the American mathematical society. July 1901. — \gesperrt{Scheibner}, Zur linearen Transformation der Theta-Funktionen und elliptischen Modulfunktionen, Berichte der Sächs. Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften. Oktober 1906.}. We wish here to follow a route which has the advantage of great simplicity, but which for that very reason contains no derivation, but only a proof of the finished formula.

For two special cases we have already carried out this determination earlier [§ 34, (4), (5)], and it is upon this result that we shall rely here. They are the formulae:
\weq{5}{\eta(\omega \pm 1) = e^{\pm\frac{\pi i}{12}}\,\eta(\omega)}
and
\weq{6}{\eta\left(-\frac{1}{\omega}\right) = \sqrt{-i\omega}\ \eta(\omega),}
where $\sqrt{-i\omega}$ is to be taken \gesperrt{with positive real part}.

We now set
\weq{7}{\frac{\eta\left(\dfrac{\gamma+\delta\omega}{\alpha+\beta\omega}\right)}{\eta(\omega)} = E\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\alpha, & \beta\\ \gamma, & \delta\end{smallmatrix}, \omega\right),}
and have to determine the value of this symbol. By (5), (6), (7) we have:
\weq{8}{E\left(\begin{smallmatrix}-\alpha, & -\beta\\ -\gamma, & -\delta\end{smallmatrix}, \omega\right) = E\left(\begin{smallmatrix}\alpha, & \beta\\ \gamma, & \delta\end{smallmatrix}, \omega\right),}
\weq{9}{E\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1, & 0\\ 0, & 1\end{smallmatrix}, \omega\right) = 1,}
\weq{10}{E\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1, & 0\\ \pm 1, & 1\end{smallmatrix}, \omega\right) = e^{\pm\frac{\pi i}{12}},}
\weq{11}{E\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0, & 1\\ -1, & 0\end{smallmatrix}, \omega\right) = \sqrt{-i\omega}.}
