Accordingly there results from (7), if we separate off the term
corresponding to the value $\nu = 0$ and replace the negative $\nu$ by
$-\nu$:
\[
\frac{\vartheta'_{11}\vartheta_{11}(v+a)}{\pi\vartheta_{11}(v)\vartheta_{11}(a)} = \frac{2i}{e^{2\pi iv} - 1} + \frac{2ie^{2\pi ia}}{e^{2\pi ia} - 1}
\]
\[
+ 2i\sum_{1,\infty}^{\nu} \frac{e^{-2\pi i\nu a}}{q^{-2\nu}e^{2\pi iv} - 1} + 2i\sum_{1,\infty}^{\nu} \frac{q^{2\nu}e^{2\pi i\nu a}e^{2\pi iv}}{q^{2\nu}e^{2\pi iv} - 1}.
\]
But we have
\[
\frac{2i}{e^{2\pi iv} - 1} = \frac{e^{-\pi iv}}{\sin\pi v} = \mathrm{cotg}\,\pi v - i,
\]
\[
\frac{2ie^{2\pi ia}}{e^{2\pi ia} - 1} = \frac{e^{\pi ia}}{\sin\pi a} = \mathrm{cotg}\,\pi a + i,
\]
and accordingly this development can also be represented thus:
\weq{14}{\frac{\vartheta'_{11}\vartheta_{11}(v+a)}{\pi\vartheta_{11}(v)\vartheta_{11}(a)} = \mathrm{cotg}\,\pi v + \mathrm{cotg}\,\pi a
-2i\sum \left(\frac{q^m e^{m\pi ia}}{e^{-2\pi iv} - q^m} - \frac{q^m e^{-m\pi ia}}{e^{2\pi iv} - q^m}\right),}
in which $m$ runs through the \gesperrt{sequence of the even numbers}
\[
m = 2, 4, 6, 8, \dots
\]
The domain of validity of this development is
\[
-\omega'' < a'' < \omega''.
\]

From these formulae three others can be derived, by increasing
$v$ or $a$ or both by $\frac{1}{2}$. Three others, however, which correspond to the
increase of $v$ and $a$ by $\frac{1}{2}\omega$, must be derived directly.
They exhibit real form when $q$, $a$, $v$ are real.
