\begin{center}
\textbf{§ 143.\quad \gesperrt{Partial norms of $f(\omega)$.}}
\end{center}

We make one further application of the limit formula (30) of § 141 to the
determination of the product
\weq{1}{\Pi f(\omega),}
in which the product sign $\Pi$ extends not over a complete system of
roots, but only over the roots $\omega$ of the classes of forms of one
genus. We restrict ourselves here, however, to the case in which
\weq{2}{\Delta = -4m}
is a \gesperrt{fundamental discriminant}, so that $m$ has no square
divisor and is either $\equiv 1$ or $\equiv 2 \pmod 4$, or, what is the
same thing,
\weq{3}{\Delta \equiv -4 \ \text{or} \ \equiv 8 \pmod{16}.}
Let $\delta$ be a \gesperrt{fundamental divisor} of $\Delta$ different from
$\Delta$ and 1, and let $\chi(\delta, k)$ be the character of the class $k$
corresponding to this fundamental divisor.

% [sic] The second delta is printed lowercase; the complementary divisor belongs to Delta, as delta delta'=Delta immediately below confirms.
If $\delta'$ is the fundamental divisor to $\delta$ that is complementary
to $\delta$, then in this case
\[
\begin{aligned}
\delta\,\delta' &= \Delta, \\
\chi(\delta, k) &= \chi(\delta', k),
\end{aligned}
\]
and we thus obtain all the genera if we put for $\delta$ the \gesperrt{odd
fundamental divisors}, which is what we shall do here. Now let, as in
§ 141, (29), (30):
\weq{4}{\begin{aligned}
\psi_0 &= \Bigl(2, 2, \frac{m+1}{2}\Bigr), \ \Bigl(2, 0, \frac{m}{2}\Bigr), \\
\psi &= (A, 2B, C), \\
\psi' &= \psi_0\,\psi.
\end{aligned}}
