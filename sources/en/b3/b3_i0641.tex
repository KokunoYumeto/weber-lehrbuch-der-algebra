We can generate the field $\mathfrak{K}''$ by means of a number:
\weq{11}{\xi'' = \alpha\xi + \alpha'\xi',}
in which $\alpha, \alpha'$ are integers in $\Omega$.

Let $\mathfrak{P}''$ be a prime ideal in $\mathfrak{K}''$ dividing some $\mathfrak{p}_1$, and let
$\mathfrak{P}, \mathfrak{P}'$ be the prime ideals in $\mathfrak{K}, \mathfrak{K}'$ divisible by $\mathfrak{P}''$, and $N(\mathfrak{p}_1) = p$.
Then by 9.:
\weq{12}{\xi^p \equiv \xi \ (\mathrm{mod}\ \mathfrak{P}), \quad \xi'^p \equiv \xi' \ (\mathrm{mod}\ \mathfrak{P}'),}
and consequently, by (11):
\weq{13}{\xi''^p \equiv \alpha^p \xi^p + \alpha'^p \xi'^p \equiv \xi'' \ (\mathrm{mod}\ \mathfrak{P}'').}
If conversely the congruence (13) is satisfied, then the congruences (12)
follow from it again, because $\xi$ and $\xi'$ can be expressed rationally
[in the form (9)] by means of $\xi''$.

Thus $\mathfrak{K}''$ is likewise a class field, and its degree is just as
high as that of $\mathfrak{K}$ and $\mathfrak{K}'$. Hence $\xi$ and $\xi'$ are primitive
numbers of $\mathfrak{K}''$, and consequently all these fields are identical (Bd.\,I,
§ 151, 3.). If $\mathfrak{K}$ is a class field, then the fields conjugate to $\mathfrak{K}$
are also class fields and are therefore identical with $\mathfrak{K}$.

With this the theorem is proved:

10. \gesperrt{For a given number group there is only one class field, and this is a normal field.}

We now wish to become more closely acquainted with the most important properties and
applications of the class field in the cases arising from complex
multiplication.

Let us assume that the number group $A$ contains another group $A'$ as a divisor of
finite index $(A, A')$, and that these two groups
possess class fields:
\weq{14}{\begin{aligned} \mathfrak{K} &= \mathfrak{K}(A) = \Omega(\xi), \\ \mathfrak{K}' &= \mathfrak{K}(A') = \Omega(\xi'), \end{aligned}}
then it can be proved that the field $\mathfrak{K}$ is contained in $\mathfrak{K}'$. For if
$\mathfrak{p}_1, \mathfrak{p}_1'$ are the prime ideals of the first degree in $\Omega$ of the
principal classes $\overline{A}_1, \overline{A}_1'$, then the $\mathfrak{p}_1'$ are contained
among the $\mathfrak{p}_1$. If therefore $p$ is the prime which $\mathfrak{p}_1$ and $\mathfrak{p}_1'$ divide,
then by 9.:
\weq{15}{\begin{aligned} \xi^p &\equiv \xi \ (\mathrm{mod}\ \mathfrak{P}) \text{ for all } \mathfrak{p}_1 \text{ and consequently for all } \mathfrak{p}_1', \\ \xi'^p &\equiv \xi' \ (\mathrm{mod}\ \mathfrak{P}') \text{ for all } \mathfrak{p}_1', \end{aligned}}
if $\mathfrak{P}$ and $\mathfrak{P}'$ are prime ideals of the first degree in $\mathfrak{K}$ and $\mathfrak{K}'$.
