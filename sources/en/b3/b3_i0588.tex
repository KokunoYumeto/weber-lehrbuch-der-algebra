\sum \frac{\omega}{y^2\omega^2 - x^2} &= S_1 \qquad x, y \text{ odd}, \\
&= S_2 \qquad x \text{ odd}, \ y \text{ even}, \\

$x$, $y$ positive and relatively prime, then it follows:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\beta\,S'_{10} &= -2\,i\,\omega + 4\,i\,S_2,\\
\beta\,S'_{01} &= +\frac{2\,i}{\omega} + 4\,i\,S_3,\\
\beta\,S'_{11} &= 4\,i\,S_1,
\end{aligned}
\]
and consequently there results from (10), for a purely imaginary $\omega$:
\weq{12}{\begin{aligned}
\log f(\omega) &= \tfrac{1}{3}\log\sqrt{2} + \frac{\pi i}{24}\left(-\omega + \frac{1}{\omega} - 4\,S_1 + 2\,S_2 + 2\,S_3\right), \\
\log f_1(\omega) &= \tfrac{1}{3}\log\sqrt{2} + \frac{\pi i}{24}\left(-\omega - \frac{2}{\omega} + 2\,S_1 + 2\,S_2 - 4\,S_3\right), \\
\log f_2(\omega) &= \tfrac{1}{3}\log\sqrt{2} + \frac{\pi i}{24}\left(2\,\omega + \frac{1}{\omega} + 2\,S_1 - 4\,S_2 + 2\,S_3\right).
\end{aligned}}

\begin{center}\textbf{\S\,148. Elementary Derivation of the Developments.}\end{center}

One can also arrive at the foregoing developments by the following route. By a known
formula of analysis:

\weq{1}{\sum_{1,\infty}^{x} \frac{\omega}{\omega^2 y^2 - x^2} = -\frac{1}{2\,\omega y^2} + \frac{\pi i}{2\,y}\,\frac{q^{2y}+1}{q^{2y}-1},}

if $q = e^{\pi i \omega}$$^{1)}$\begingroup\renewcommand{\thefootnote}{1)}\footnotetext[1]{From the development:
\[
\mathrm{cotg}\ z = \frac{1}{z} + \frac{2\,z}{z^2-\pi^2} + \frac{2\,z}{z^2-4\pi^2} + \frac{2\,z}{z^2-9\pi^2} + \cdots
\]}\endgroup. From this, by development in powers of $q$:

\weq{2}{\sum_{1,\infty}^{x} \frac{\omega}{\omega^2 y^2 - x^2} = -\frac{1}{2\,\omega y^2} - \frac{\pi i}{y}\left(\frac{1}{2} + \sum_{1,\infty}^{n} q^{2ny}\right).}

If one here replaces $y$ by $\tfrac{1}{2}y$, it follows:

\weq{3}{\sum_{1,\infty}^{x} \frac{2\,\omega}{\omega^2 y^2 - 4x^2} = -\frac{1}{\omega y^2} - \frac{\pi i}{y}\left(\frac{1}{2} + \sum_{1,\infty} q^{ny}\right),}

and if one subtracts (2) from (3):

\weq{4}{\sum \frac{(-1)^x\,\omega}{\omega^2 y^2 - x^2} = -\frac{1}{2\,\omega y^2} - \frac{\pi i}{y}\sum_{1,\infty}^{x} q^{(2n-1)y}.}
% [sic] The right sum in(4) is printed with index x although its summand uses n. Printed index retained.
