The expansions in powers of $q$ for $A$ and $B$ result
from (14), so far as they are needed for the calculation,
as follows:
\[
\begin{aligned}
n &= 3, & A &= q^{-\frac{1}{2}}(1 - 5q \dots), \\
& & B &= q^{-\frac{1}{2}}(1 - 5q \dots), \\
n &= 5, & A &= q^{-\frac{1}{2}}(1 - 2q \dots), \\
& & B &= q^{-\frac{1}{2}}(1 - 2q \dots), \\
n &= 7, & A &= q^{-1}(1 - 4q \dots), \\
& & B &= q^{-1}(1 + 3q \dots), \\
n &= 11, & A &= q^{-\frac{5}{2}}(1 - 6q + 21q^2 \dots), \\
& & B &= q^{-\frac{1}{2}}(1 - q + 2q^2 \dots), \\
n &= 13, & A &= q^{-\frac{1}{2}}(1 + 2q^2 - 2q^3 \dots), \\
& & B &= q^{-\frac{7}{2}}(1 + 6q + 15q^2 + 26q^3 \dots), \\
n &= 17, & A &= q^{-2}(1 - 3q + 6q^2 - 13q^3 + 25q^4 - 39q^5 + 76q^6 \dots), \\
& & B &= q^{-3}(1 + 4q + 6q^2 + 8q^3 + 17q^4 + 28q^5 + 54q^6 \dots), \\
n &= 19, & A &= q^{-\frac{3}{2}}(1 - 2q + 3q^2 - 5q^3 + 11q^4 - 13q^5 + 24q^6 - 28q^7 \dots), \\
& & B &= q^{-\frac{5}{2}}(1 + 3q + 3q^2 + 4q^3 + 9q^4 + 4q^5 + 39q^6 - 27q^7 \dots),
\end{aligned}
\]
and from these one obtains, by elimination of the negative powers,
the required equations between $A$ and $B$:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{I.}\quad n &= 3, & A - B &= 0, \\
n &= 5, & A - B &= 0, \\
n &= 7, & A - B + 7 &= 0, \\
n &= 11, & A - B^5 + B^3 + 2B &= 0, \\
n &= 13, & A^7 + 6A^5 + A^3 - 20A - B &= 0, \\
n &= 17, & A^3 - B^2 + 17AB - 34A^2 + 34B + 116A \\
& & + 440 &= 0, \\
n &= 19, & A^5 - B^3 + 19AB^2 - 95A^2B + 109A^3 \\
& & + 128B - 128A &= 0\ ^{1}).
\end{aligned}
\]
$^{1}$) These equations were first set up by \gesperrt{Schlaefli} (Journal für
Mathematik, Bd. 72).
