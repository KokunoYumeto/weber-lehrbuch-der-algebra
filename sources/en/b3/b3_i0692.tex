1. \gesperrt{Accordingly every function $\omega$ can be represented linearly with
rational coefficients in $z_1$ by the $n_1$ functions:}

\weq{6}{z^r\theta^s \quad \begin{aligned} r &= 0, 1, \ldots, e_1-1 \\ s &= 0, 1, \ldots, f-1 \end{aligned},}
\gesperrt{which we denote, in some order, by}

\weq{7}{\eta_1, \eta_2, \ldots, \eta_{n_1}}
\gesperrt{and these functions are linearly independent, i.\,e. there
exists between them no linear relation with rational
coefficients in $z_1$:}
\[
x_1\eta_1 + x_2\eta_2 + \cdots + x_{n_1}\eta_{n_1} = 0,
\]
\gesperrt{except when the $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_{n_1}$ all vanish.}

For $f$ is the lowest degree of an equation for $\theta$ with
rational coefficients in $z$ and $z_1$ (\S\ 172).

Let us now consider the functional

\weq{8}{\tau_1 = t_1\eta_1 + t_2\eta_2 + \cdots + t_{n_1}\eta_{n_1}}
with the functional variables $t_1, t_2, \ldots, t_{n_1}$; there then results
a system of equations:

\weq{9}{\tau_1^r = x_{1,r}\eta_1 + x_{2,r}\eta_2 + \cdots + x_{n_1,r}\eta_{n_1},}
in which $r$ may be any natural number, including 0, and the $x_{i,r}$
are rational holomorphic functionals in $z_1$. From this there results,
by elimination of the $\eta$, an equation

\weq{10}{\Phi(\tau_1) = \tau_1^m + A_1\tau_1^{m-1} + \cdots + A_{m-1}\tau_1 + A_m = 0}
of degree at most $n_1$, with rational functionals in $z_1$ as
coefficients.
