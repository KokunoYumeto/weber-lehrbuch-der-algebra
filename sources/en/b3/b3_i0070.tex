\begin{center}
\textbf{\S\,14. \gesperrt{Origin of the elliptic functions.}}
\end{center}

If in the elliptic differential of the first kind in the \gesperrt{Legendre} normal form
\[
\frac{1}{2}\frac{dz}{\sqrt{z(1-z)(1-\varkappa^2 z)}}
\]
the substitution
\weq{1}{z = \sin^2\varphi}
is made, and the integration is then carried out from $\varphi = 0$ onwards, there arises the elliptic integral of the first kind
\weq{2}{\int_0^\varphi \frac{d\varphi}{\sqrt{1-\varkappa^2\sin^2\varphi}} = u.}
\gesperrt{Jacobi} calls the upper limit $\varphi$ of this integral its \gesperrt{amplitude} and writes
\weq{3}{\varphi = \mathrm{am}\,u.}
The trigonometric functions of this arc
\weq{4}{\sin\varphi,\ \cos\varphi,\ \sqrt{1-\varkappa^2\sin^2\varphi} = \Delta\varphi}
are now those which, regarded as functions of $u$, are called \gesperrt{elliptic functions} and are denoted by \gesperrt{Jacobi} as
\[
\mathrm{sinam}\,u,\quad \mathrm{cosam}\,u,\quad \Delta\,\mathrm{am}\,u,
\]
or, following \gesperrt{Gudermann}, more briefly as follows:
\weq{5}{\mathrm{sn}\,u,\quad \mathrm{cn}\,u,\quad \mathrm{dn}\,u}
These functions depend on the two arguments $u$ and $\varkappa^2$, and when a more exact notation is necessary, one also writes
\[
\mathrm{sn}\,(u,\varkappa^2),\quad \mathrm{cn}\,(u,\varkappa^2),\quad \mathrm{dn}\,(u,\varkappa^2)
\]
instead.
