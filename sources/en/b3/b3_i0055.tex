\begin{center}
2. \gesperrt{The Landen Transformation.}
\end{center}

\[
V = a\eta(\xi-\eta), \qquad U = \xi(\xi-\varkappa^2\eta).
\]

The conditions that $U-V$, $U-\lambda^2V$ be squares read

\[
4a = (\varkappa^2+a)^2, \qquad 4a\lambda^2 = (\varkappa^2+a\lambda^2)^2,
\]

from which

\[
\lambda(\varkappa^2+a) = \varkappa^2+a\lambda^2,
\]

or, divided by $\lambda-1$,

\[
a\lambda = \varkappa^2;
\]

this, substituted into one of the above equations, gives

\[
4\lambda = \varkappa^2(1+\lambda)^2,
\]

and by the solution of this quadratic equation, when
$\varkappa'=\sqrt{1-\varkappa^2}$ is set:

\[
\lambda = \frac{1-\varkappa'}{1+\varkappa'}, \qquad a=(1+\varkappa')^2,
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
U-V &= [\xi-(1+\varkappa')\eta]^2,\\
U-\lambda^2V &= [\xi-(1-\varkappa')\eta]^2,\\
H &= (1+\varkappa')^2[\xi-(1+\varkappa')\eta][\xi-(1-\varkappa')\eta],\\
T &= (1+\varkappa')[(\xi-\eta)^2-\varkappa'^2\eta^2],\\
M &= \frac{1}{1+\varkappa'},
\end{aligned}
\]

so that by the substitution

\weq{4}{z=\frac{(1+\varkappa')^2\zeta(1-\zeta)}{1-\varkappa^2\zeta}, \qquad
\lambda=\frac{1-\varkappa'}{1+\varkappa'}}

the transformation:

\weq{5}{\frac{dz}{\sqrt{z(1-z)(1-\lambda^2z)}} =
\frac{(1+\varkappa')\,d\zeta}{\sqrt{\zeta(1-\zeta)(1-\varkappa^2\zeta)}}.}

If one applies to the left-hand side of this equation once more the
\gesperrt{Gauss} transformation, replacing in the formulae (2), (3)
$\zeta,\varkappa$ by $z,\lambda$ and $z$ by a variable $\eta$, then, as
follows from (2) and (4), $\lambda$ in (3) is to be replaced by
$\varkappa$, and by combining (5) and (3) one finds:

\weq{6}{\frac{d\eta}{\sqrt{\eta(1-\eta)(1-\varkappa^2\eta)}} =
\frac{2\,d\zeta}{\sqrt{\zeta(1-\zeta)(1-\varkappa^2\zeta)}},}

and the combination of (2) and (4) yields, between the variables $\eta$
and $\zeta$, the connection:

\weq{7}{\eta = \frac{4\zeta(1-\zeta)(1-\varkappa^2\zeta)}{(1-\varkappa^2\zeta^2)^2}.}
