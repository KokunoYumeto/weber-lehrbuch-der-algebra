In Table V the four formulae which arise in this way are collected. If one subtracts formula (3) of this table from the three remaining ones, there result developments for the logarithmic derivatives of the three fundamental elliptic functions, of which we will cite one:
\weq{4}{\frac{d\log\operatorname{sn}2Ku}{\pi\,du} = \frac{d\log\sin\pi u}{\pi\,du} + 4\sin 2\pi u\sum_{h=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^h q^h}{1-2q^h\cos 2\pi u+q^{2h}}}
\[
= \frac{d\log\sin\pi u}{\pi\,du} - 4\sum_{h=1}^{\infty}\frac{q^h\sin 2h\pi u}{1+q^h}.
\]

Developments for the transcendents \gesperrt{of the third kind} result when one integrates the formulae of the fifth table between the limits $u+v$, $u-v$. In this way one obtains, e.\,g., from formula (3) of this table:
\weq{5}{\frac{1}{2}\log\frac{\theta[2K(u-v)]}{\theta[2K(u+v)]} = -4\sum\frac{q^{\frac{m}{2}}}{m(1-q^m)}\sin m\pi u\sin m\pi v.}

If one puts $u=0$ in the formulae of Tables IV and V, the following developments result:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{2K}{\pi} &= 1+4\sum\frac{q^{\frac{m}{2}}}{1+q^m} \phantom{,} = 1+4\sum\frac{(-1)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}q^n}{1-q^n}, \\
\frac{2\varkappa K}{\pi} &= \phantom{1+{}} 4\sum\frac{q^{\frac{n}{2}}}{1+q^n} \phantom{,} = \phantom{1+{}} 4\sum\frac{(-1)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}q^{\frac{n}{2}}}{1-q^n}, \\
\frac{2\varkappa'K}{\pi} &= 1+4\sum\frac{(-1)^{\frac{m}{2}}q^{\frac{m}{2}}}{1+q^m} = 1-4\sum\frac{(-1)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}q^n}{1+q^n}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Finally one obtains a development for the complete integral of the second kind $E$ from the formula
\[
Z(v) = E(v) - \frac{E}{K}v.
\]
If in this, after the differentiation, one puts $v=0$, it follows that:
\[
Z'(0) = \frac{K-E}{K},
\]
and hence, by Table V, formula (3):
\[
K-E = \frac{2\pi^2}{K}\sum\frac{q^n}{(1-q^n)^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{K}\sum\frac{mq^{\frac{m}{2}}}{1-q^m}.
\]
