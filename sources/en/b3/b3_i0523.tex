In \S\,73 we investigated the influence of the three permutations

\weq{4}{\begin{aligned}
(c') &= (\omega, \omega+2), \\
(c'') &= \left(\omega, \frac{-1}{\omega}\right), \\
(c''') &= \left(\omega, \frac{\omega-1}{\omega+1}\right),
\end{aligned}}

by which $u$ passes over into

\[
e^{-\frac{\pi i}{12}}u, \quad u, \quad \frac{\sqrt{2}}{u},
\]
upon the roots of the equations (2) and (3). This influence results from the compositions:

\weq{5}{\begin{pmatrix} n, & 0 \\ c, & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1, & 0 \\ 2, & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} n, & 0 \\ c', & 1 \end{pmatrix},}

\weq{6}{c' \equiv c + 2 \ (\mathrm{mod}\ n);}

\weq{7}{\begin{pmatrix} n, & 0 \\ c, & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 0, & 1 \\ -1, & 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -c'', & n \\ -\dfrac{c\,c''+1}{n}, & c \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} n, & 0 \\ c'', & 1 \end{pmatrix},}

\weq{8}{c\,c'' \equiv -1 \ (\mathrm{mod}\ n);}

\weq{9}{\begin{pmatrix} n, & 0 \\ c, & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1, & 1 \\ -1, & 1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \alpha, & \beta \\ \gamma, & \delta \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 1, & 1 \\ -1, & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} n, & 0 \\ c''', & 1 \end{pmatrix},}

\weq{10}{c''' \equiv \frac{c-1}{c+1} \ (\mathrm{mod}\ n),}

\[
\begin{gathered}
\alpha - \beta = 1 - c''', \quad \gamma + \delta = c + 1, \\
\alpha + \beta = n, \quad n\,(\gamma - \delta) = (c-1) - c'''\,(c+1),
\end{gathered}
\]
and hence, by \S\,34 and \S\,40, (12), the root $v_c$ passes, under the substitutions $(c')$, $(c'')$, $(c''')$ and with regard to

\[
2 \equiv 2\,n^2 \ (\mathrm{mod}\ 48),
\]
over into:

\weq{11}{e^{-\frac{\pi i n}{12}} v_{c'}, \quad v_{c''}, \quad \left(\frac{2}{n}\right)\frac{\sqrt{2}}{v_{c'''}},}

and this holds also for $c = \infty$. The permutations of the indices which result in this way are:

\weq{12}{\begin{aligned}
n &= 7, & c &= \infty, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, \\
& & c' &= \infty, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 0, 1, \\
& & c'' &= 0, \infty, 6, 3, 2, 5, 4, 1, \\
& & c''' &= 1, 6, 0, 5, 4, 2, 3, \infty.
\end{aligned}}
