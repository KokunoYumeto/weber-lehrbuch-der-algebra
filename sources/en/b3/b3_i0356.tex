4. \gesperrt{If in a normal field the same class of forms belongs to the two ideals $\mathfrak{a}$ and $\mathfrak{a}'$, then among the ideals conjugate to $\mathfrak{a}'$ there is one that is equivalent to $\mathfrak{a}$.}

\begin{center}
\textbf{§ 89. Composition of forms and multiplication of ideals.}
\end{center}

Let $\mathfrak{a}$ and $\mathfrak{b}$ now be two ideals in $\Omega$, and let
\weq{1}{\mathfrak{c} = \mathfrak{a}\mathfrak{b}}
be the product formed from them both. Let further
\weq{2}{\begin{aligned}
\mathfrak{a} &= (\alpha_1,\ \alpha_2,\ \ldots,\ \alpha_n), \\
\mathfrak{b} &= (\beta_1,\ \beta_2,\ \ldots,\ \beta_n), \\
\mathfrak{c} &= (\gamma_1,\ \gamma_2,\ \ldots,\ \gamma_n)
\end{aligned}}
be bases of these ideals. Since every number $\alpha_h\beta_k$ belongs to $\mathfrak{c}$, there is a system of rational integers $c^{(r)}_{hk}$ such that
\weq{3}{\alpha_h\beta_k = \overset{r}{\Sigma}\, c^{(r)}_{h,k}\,\gamma_r}
holds; and since every number in $\mathfrak{c}$ arises by the multiplication of one number from $\mathfrak{a}$ and one from $\mathfrak{b}$ and the addition of these products (Bd.\,II, § 169), conversely also
\weq{4}{\gamma_s = \overset{h,k}{\Sigma}\, a^{(s)}_{h,k}\,\alpha_h\beta_k,}
in which the $a^{(s)}_{h,k}$ are likewise rational integers.

If one substitutes (3) into (4), it follows that
\weq{5}{\gamma_s = \overset{r}{\Sigma}\,\gamma_r \overset{h,k}{\Sigma}\, a^{(s)}_{h,k}\,c^{(r)}_{h,k},}
and from this
\weq{6}{\overset{h,k}{\Sigma}\, a^{(s)}_{h,k}\,c^{(r)}_{h,k} = (r,s),}
in which
\[
(r,r) = 1, \quad (r,s) = 0, \quad r \neq s.
\]
