In the paper of \gesperrt{Kiepert} mentioned above the general equation of the fifth degree is transformed into the form (15), for which only the solution of a quadratic equation is required. If one regards $a, b, c$ therein as arbitrarily given, then the equations (16) serve for the determination of $\lambda, \mu, \gamma_3$. This likewise is done with the help of a quadratic equation, though this is not to be seen at first glance. (Cf. F. \gesperrt{Klein}, Vorlesungen über das Ikosa\"eder, S. 191 f.)

We shall return further on to the formation of resolvents for the seventh transformation degree, when we have at our disposal the means which complex multiplication offers us.
