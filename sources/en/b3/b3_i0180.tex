The direct transformation of the three expressions (7), (8), (9) into one
 another is most easily accomplished if one starts from the formula last
given by \gesperrt{Jacobi}:
\weq{10}{1 - \varkappa^2 \operatorname{sn}(a+u)\operatorname{sn}(a-u)\operatorname{sn}(a+v)\operatorname{sn}(a-v) = \dfrac{(1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^4 a)(1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^2u\operatorname{sn}^2v)}{(1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^2a\operatorname{sn}^2u)(1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^2a\operatorname{sn}^2v)}.}
The verification of this formula is easy for this reason, that
with the help of \S\,44, (13) one obtains on the right and on the left \gesperrt{rational functions}
of $\operatorname{sn}^2u$, $\operatorname{sn}^2v$, whose identity is immediately evident.

If in this formula one replaces
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
u, & v, & a
\end{array}
\]
by
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
\dfrac{u-v}{2}, & \dfrac{u+v}{2}\pm a, & \dfrac{u+v}{2},
\end{array}
\]
there results
\[
\begin{gathered}
1 \pm \varkappa^2 \operatorname{sn} u\operatorname{sn} v\operatorname{sn} a\operatorname{sn}(u+v\pm a) \\
= \dfrac{\left[1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^4\left(\dfrac{u+v}{2}\right)\right]\left[1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^2\left(\dfrac{u-v}{2}\right)\operatorname{sn}^2\left(\dfrac{u+v}{2}\pm a\right)\right]}{\left[1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^2\left(\dfrac{u+v}{2}\right)\operatorname{sn}^2\dfrac{u-v}{2}\right]\left[1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^2\left(\dfrac{u+v}{2}\right)\operatorname{sn}^2\left(\dfrac{u+v}{2}\pm a\right)\right]},
\end{gathered}
\]
and if one divides the two formulae contained herein by one
 another, there results the agreement of (8)
and (9).

If one puts $v=u$ in (10), there results:
\weq{11}{1 - \varkappa^2 \operatorname{sn}^2(a+u)\operatorname{sn}^2(a-u) = \dfrac{(1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^4a)(1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^4u)}{(1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^2a\operatorname{sn}^2u)^2}.}
If one replaces herein
\[
\begin{array}{cc}
a & u
\end{array}
\]
first by
\[
\begin{array}{cc}
\dfrac{u+v}{2}\pm a, & -\dfrac{u+v}{2},
\end{array}
\]
and then by
\[
\begin{array}{cc}
\dfrac{u+v}{2}\pm a, & \dfrac{u-v}{2},
\end{array}
\]
there result four formulae:
\[
1 - \varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^2a\operatorname{sn}^2(u+v\pm a) = \dfrac{\left[1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^4\left(\dfrac{u+v}{2}\pm a\right)\right]\left(1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^4\dfrac{u+v}{2}\right)}{\left[1-\varkappa^2\operatorname{sn}^2\left(\dfrac{u+v}{2}\pm a\right)\operatorname{sn}^2\dfrac{u+v}{2}\right]^2},
\]
