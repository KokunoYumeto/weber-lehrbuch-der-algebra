\gesperrt{This is Abel's theorem for the differential
 quotients of the first kind.}

To show that this requirement on the $y_i$ is also sufficient for the
 fulfilment of b), we regard the functions in $\Omega$
as functions of $z' = 1:z$. For these,
\[
\lambda_1' = z'^{r_1}\lambda_1,\quad \lambda_2' = z'^{r_2}\lambda_2,\quad \ldots,\quad \lambda_n' = z'^{r_n}\lambda_n
\]
form a minimal basis by § 193, 6., and
\[
\mu_1' = z^{r_1}\mu_1,\quad \mu_2' = z^{r_2}\mu_2,\quad \ldots,\quad \mu_n' = z^{r_n}\mu_n
\]
is by (10) the basis complementary to it. Consequently $z'\mu' = 0$
for $z' = 0$ [by a), applied to $z'$], and hence
\[
z^{r_i-1}\mu_i = 0,
\]
\[
zy_i\mu_i = \frac{y_i}{z^{i-2}}z^{r_i-1}\mu_i = 0 \text{ for } z = \infty, % [sic] printed with dropped r in denominator exponent; expected r_i-2
\]
if the degree of $y_i$ is not higher than $(r_i-1)$. % [sic] German source prints $r_i-1$; the preceding and following derivation require $r_i-2$

It has thereby been shown that all functions contained in the
 form (19), in which the $y_i$ are integral rational functions
of $z$ of degree at most $r_i-2$, are differential quotients
of the first kind, and that conversely all differential quotients
of the first kind can be represented in this form, and
the principal theorem follows:

1. \gesperrt{The class of differentials of the first kind
is of the dimension}
\[
(O,W) = (r_2-1)+(r_3-1)+\cdots+(r_n-1) = p.
\]

From this the following further results: if $A$ is any
class of polygons of order $a$ and $W$ the principal class of the
first kind, then by § 192, (14):
\[
(A, W) \mathrel{\underset{>}{=}} (O, W) - a = p - a, % source prints equality bars over a greater-than wedge
\]
hence, if $a \mathrel{\underset{<}{=}} p - 1$: % source prints equality bars over a less-than wedge
\[
(A, W) \mathrel{\underset{>}{=}} 1. % source prints equality bars over a greater-than wedge
\]

Hence, if $a$ is less than $p$, there are always polygons of the first
kind that are divisible by $\mathfrak{A}$, and from this it follows:

2. \gesperrt{Every polygon of the $(p-1)$th and lower
order is of the first kind.}

By § 192, 5., if $m$ is not greater than $p$, one can choose
an $m$-gon $\mathfrak{M}$ so that, if $M$ is the class determined by $\mathfrak{M}$,
\[
(M, W) = p - m
\]
