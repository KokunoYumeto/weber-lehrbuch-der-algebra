\weq{1}{\begin{array}{l}
\sigma(v,\,2K,\,2iK') = \displaystyle\sum_{0,\infty}^{\nu} A_\nu\,\dfrac{v^{2\nu+1}}{(2\nu+1)!},\\[8pt]
\sigma_{00}(v,\,2K,\,2iK') = \displaystyle\sum_{0,\infty}^{\nu} B_\nu\,\dfrac{v^{2\nu}}{(2\nu)!},\\[8pt]
\sigma_{01}(v,\,2K,\,2iK') = \displaystyle\sum_{0,\infty}^{\nu} C_\nu\,\dfrac{v^{2\nu}}{(2\nu)!},\\[8pt]
\sigma_{10}(v,\,2K,\,2iK') = \displaystyle\sum_{0,\infty}^{\nu} D_\nu\,\dfrac{v^{2\nu}}{(2\nu)!},
\end{array}}
and the $A_\nu$, $B_\nu$, $C_\nu$, $D_\nu$ are the functions of $\varkappa^2$, or of $\omega$, that are to be determined. If we regard them first as functions of $\omega$, then the linear transformations [\S\,45, (3)] give:
\weq{2}{\begin{array}{l}
A_\nu\left(-\dfrac{1}{\omega}\right) = (-1)^\nu A_\nu(\omega),\\[6pt]
B_\nu\left(-\dfrac{1}{\omega}\right) = (-1)^\nu B_\nu(\omega),\\[6pt]
C_\nu\left(-\dfrac{1}{\omega}\right) = (-1)^\nu D_\nu(\omega),\\[6pt]
D_\nu\left(-\dfrac{1}{\omega}\right) = (-1)^\nu C_\nu(\omega).
\end{array}}
\weq{3}{\begin{array}{l}
\varkappa'^{2\nu} A_\nu(\omega+1) = A_\nu(\omega),\\[4pt]
\varkappa'^{2\nu} B_\nu(\omega+1) = C_\nu(\omega),\\[4pt]
\varkappa'^{2\nu} C_\nu(\omega+1) = B_\nu(\omega),\\[4pt]
\varkappa'^{2\nu} D_\nu(\omega+1) = D_\nu(\omega).
\end{array}}
\weq{4}{\begin{array}{l}
A_\nu(\omega+2) = A_\nu(\omega),\\[4pt]
B_\nu(\omega+2) = B_\nu(\omega),\\[4pt]
C_\nu(\omega+2) = C_\nu(\omega),\\[4pt]
D_\nu(\omega+2) = D_\nu(\omega).
\end{array}}
From this we infer the characteristic properties of these coefficients as functions of $\varkappa^2$. It is evident at once from the meaning of the coefficients that, for every value of $\varkappa^2$ with the possible exception of $\varkappa^2 = 0, 1, \infty$, the coefficients $A_\nu(\varkappa^2)$, $B_\nu(\varkappa^2)$, $C_\nu(\varkappa^2)$, $D_\nu(\varkappa^2)$ are finite and continuous functions of $\varkappa^2$.

But these functions remain finite for $\varkappa^2 = 0$ as well, and their values can easily be found by letting $q$, and hence $\varkappa^2$, pass into zero in the representations of \S\,37.
