From (2) there follows further, with the use of (1) § 20, if one
puts $g_1' = 2$, $g_2' = 0$, or $g_1' = 0$, $g_2' = 2$:

\weq{3}{
\begin{aligned}
\vartheta_{g_1+2,\,g_2}(v) &= e^{2\pi i g_2}\,\vartheta_{g_1,g_2}(v),\\
\vartheta_{g_1,\,g_2+2}(v) &= e^{\pi i g_1}\,\vartheta_{g_1,g_2}(v),
\end{aligned}
}

and thus by (2) the periodicity of the functions
$\vartheta_{g_1,g_2}(v)$ is at the same time completely expressed. For example, if
$\mu$ and $\nu$ are integers, there follows:

\weq{4}{
\vartheta_{00}\!\left(v - \frac{(2\nu-1)\,\omega + 2\mu - 1}{2}\right)
= (-1)^{\nu+1}\, i\, e^{\frac{-\pi i \omega}{4}(2\nu-1)^2}\, e^{\pi i (2\nu-1) v}\, \vartheta_{11}(v).
}

\weq{5}{
\vartheta_{11}(v - \nu\omega - \mu) = (-1)^{\mu+\nu}\, e^{-\pi i \omega \nu^2}\, e^{2\pi i \nu v}\, \vartheta_{11}(v).
}

Formula (2) is valid for entirely arbitrary, even complex
values of $g_1, g_2$.

If in the defining formulae of the $\vartheta$-functions
[§ 20, (1)] one replaces $v$ by $-v-1$, and by $-v-\omega$, and further $g_1, g_2$ by
$-g_1, -g_2$, it appears that $\vartheta_{-g_1,-g_2}(-v)$ satisfies the same
 conditions as $\vartheta_{g_1,g_2}(v)$, and that accordingly

\[
\vartheta_{g_1,g_2}(v),\quad \vartheta_{-g_1,-g_2}(-v)
\]

are identical up to a constant factor; in particular, therefore, as follows from $v = 0$:

\[
\vartheta_{0,0}(v) = \vartheta_{0,0}(-v),
\]

and thereupon formula (1) yields, if one replaces $v, g_1, g_2$ by
$-v, -g_1, -g_2$:

\weq{6}{
\vartheta_{g_1,g_2}(v) = \vartheta_{-g_1,-g_2}(-v).
}

If the elements $g_1, g_2$ are integers, then $(g_1, g_2)$ is called
a \gesperrt{principal characteristic}. There are four essentially
different ones, namely:

\[
(0,0),\quad (0,1),\quad (1,0),\quad (1,1),
\]

and accordingly also four essentially different principal $\vartheta$-functions:

\weq{7}{
\vartheta_{00}(v),\quad \vartheta_{01}(v),\quad \vartheta_{10}(v),\quad \vartheta_{11}(v),
}

of which the first is also denoted by $\vartheta(v)$.

\gesperrt{In what follows, by characteristics and
$\vartheta$-functions only principal characteristics and
principal functions will be understood.}
