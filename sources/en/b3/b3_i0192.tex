One reaches this more simply, however, by the
application of the \gesperrt{Landen transformation}.

We bound the plane of the complex values $\varkappa^2$ by
laying two cuts along the real axis, from 0 to $-\infty$ and
from 1 to $\infty$. In the plane so bounded all
roots of $\varkappa^2, \varkappa'^2$ are then uniquely determined by the requirement that, for
positive properly fractional $\varkappa^2$, they are to be \gesperrt{real and positive}. Now
by the formulae of the \gesperrt{Landen} transformation
[\S\,44, (28)], if we denote by $\varkappa_1,\varkappa_1',K_1,K_1'$ the functions of $\omega$
that arise from $\varkappa,\varkappa',K,K'$ when $\omega$ is replaced by $2\omega$,
we have:
\weq{18}{\begin{aligned}
\varkappa_1 &= \frac{1-\varkappa'}{1+\varkappa'}, \qquad \varkappa_1' = \frac{2\sqrt{\varkappa'}}{1+\varkappa'}, \\
2K_1 &= (1+\varkappa')K, \qquad K_1' = (1+\varkappa')K'.
\end{aligned}}
