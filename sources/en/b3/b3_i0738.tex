This expression therefore gives a linear dependence among the differentials of the second kind, whose lower polygons are $\mathfrak P^2, \mathfrak P_1^2, \mathfrak P_2^2, \ldots, \mathfrak P_p^2$.

If one takes, for any positive exponent $m$:
\[
\mathfrak U = \mathfrak P^{m-1}\mathfrak P_1\mathfrak P_2\ldots\mathfrak P_p,
\]
and sets
\[
z = \frac{\mathfrak A}{\mathfrak U}, \quad dz = \frac{\mathfrak Z}{\mathfrak U^2},
\]
then $\mathfrak Z$ is divisible by $\mathfrak P^{m-2}$, $\mathfrak U^2$ by $\mathfrak P^{2m-2}$, and consequently the lower polygon of $dz$ by $\mathfrak P^m$. One then obtains a linear dependence among the differentials with the lower polygons
\[
\mathfrak P^m, \mathfrak P^{m-1}, \ldots, \mathfrak P^2, \mathfrak P_1^2, \mathfrak P_2^2, \ldots, \mathfrak P_p^2,
\]
and with that Theorem 4 is proved in general.
