But we have
\[
\begin{aligned}
\wp'(u)^2 &= 4\wp(u)^3 - g_2\wp(u) - g_3, \\
\wp''(u) &= 6\wp(u)^2 - \tfrac{1}{2}g_2, \\
\wp''(u) - \wp''(v) &= 6\left[\wp^2(u) - \wp^2(v)\right],
\end{aligned}
\]
from which one obtains:
\weq{11}{\wp(u+v) + \wp(u) + \wp(v) = \dfrac{1}{4}\left(\dfrac{\wp'(u)-\wp'(v)}{\wp(u)-\wp(v)}\right)^2,}
in agreement with formula (21), \S\,13.

Finally, by integrating the second equation (9) with
respect to $u$, one obtains further:
\weq{12}{\dfrac{1}{2}\log\dfrac{\sigma(v-u)}{\sigma(v+u)} + u\,\zeta(v) = \dfrac{1}{2}\int\limits_0^u \dfrac{\wp'(v)\,du}{\wp(u)-\wp(v)},}
by which an \gesperrt{integral of the third kind} with argument $u$
and parameter $v$ is expressed by the $\sigma$-function.

Another form of the integral of the third kind is obtained
by integrating (10):
\weq{13}{\log\dfrac{\sigma(u+v)}{\sigma(u)\sigma(v)} - u\,\zeta(v) = \dfrac{1}{2}\int \dfrac{\wp'(u)-\wp'(v)}{\wp(u)-\wp(v)}\,du.}
