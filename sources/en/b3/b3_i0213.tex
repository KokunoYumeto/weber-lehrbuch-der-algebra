If, in order to carry out an example here in fuller detail, one replaces
$v$ by $v + K$, and hence $u$ by $u + \frac{1}{2}$, then by
\S\,44, (18) $x, y, z$ pass over into $y/z$, $-\varkappa' x/z$, $\varkappa'/z$, and from
\S\,21, (8) one obtains from the first formula II, for an odd $n$:

\[
\frac{\vartheta_{00}\,\vartheta_{01}^{\,n^2-1}}{\vartheta_{10}}\,
\frac{\vartheta_{10}(nu)}{\vartheta_{01}(u)^{n^2}}
\left(\frac{\vartheta_{01}(u)}{\vartheta_{00}(u)}\right)^{n^2}
= (-1)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}\,\frac{y}{z}\,A\!\left(\frac{y^2}{z^2}\right).
\]
On the other hand, by the second equation II and by \S\,42 the left
side is equal to
\[
\frac{y}{z}\left(\frac{\sqrt{\varkappa'}}{z}\right)^{n^2-1} B(x^2),
\]
and consequently:
\[
B(x^2) = (-1)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}\left(\frac{z}{\sqrt{\varkappa'}}\right)^{n^2-1} A\!\left(\frac{y^2}{z^2}\right).
\]

If one puts here $x = 1$, $y = 0$, $z = \varkappa'$ or $x = 0$, $y = 1$,
$z = 1$, it follows that:
\[
B(1) = (-1)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}\, n\, \sqrt{\varkappa'}^{\,n^2-1}, \qquad
A(1) = (-1)^{\frac{n-1}{2}}\, \sqrt{\varkappa'}^{\,n^2-1}.
\]
A whole system of such formulae can be derived by making the
following substitutions in I and II:
% [source verified, 2026-08-22] At 300 dpi, the final-row denominators are
% varkappa y and varkappa; the adjacent x/varkappa forms are distinct.
\[
\begin{array}{cccc}
u, & x, & y, & z, \\[6pt]
u+\dfrac{1}{2}, & \dfrac{y}{z}, & \dfrac{-\varkappa' x}{z}, & \dfrac{\varkappa'}{z}, \\[10pt]
u+\dfrac{\omega}{2}, & \dfrac{1}{\varkappa x}, & \dfrac{-iz}{\varkappa x}, & \dfrac{-iy}{x}, \\[10pt]
u+\dfrac{1+\omega}{2}, & \dfrac{z}{\varkappa y}, & \dfrac{i\varkappa'}{\varkappa}\,\dfrac{1}{y}, & \dfrac{i\varkappa' x}{y}.
\end{array}
\]
In this way one obtains:
\weq{4}{
\begin{array}{ll}
\left(\dfrac{z}{\sqrt{\varkappa'}}\right)^{n^2-4}\! A\!\left(\dfrac{y^2}{z^2}\right) = (-1)^{\frac{n}{2}-1} A(x^2), & A(1) = (-1)^{\frac{n}{2}-1}\, n\, \sqrt{\varkappa'}^{\,n^2-4}, \\[8pt]
\left(\dfrac{z}{\sqrt{\varkappa'}}\right)^{n^2}\! B\!\left(\dfrac{y^2}{z^2}\right) = (-1)^{\frac{n}{2}} B(x^2), & B(1) = (-1)^{\frac{n}{2}}\, \sqrt{\varkappa'}^{\,n^2}, \\[8pt]
\left(\dfrac{z}{\sqrt{\varkappa'}}\right)^{n^2}\! C\!\left(\dfrac{y^2}{z^2}\right) = C(x^2), & C(1) = \sqrt{\varkappa'}^{\,n^2}, \\[8pt]
\left(\dfrac{z}{\sqrt{\varkappa'}}\right)^{n^2}\! D\!\left(\dfrac{y^2}{z^2}\right) = D(x^2), & D(1) = \sqrt{\varkappa'}^{\,n^2}, \\[8pt]
& n \text{ even.}
\end{array}
}
